- Use language that aligns with our positive tone
- Avoid confrontation in public spaces

Allowed:

Last Updated: December 14, 2025

Our Commitment
Good Energy respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

- Account Information: Email address; Password (encrypted); Avatar configuration (stored as data, not photos)

What We Don't Collect:
